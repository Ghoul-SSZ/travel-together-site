# Delete your Travel Together account

**Travel Together** by Steven Shidi Zhou · Last updated 12 October 2026

## In the app

1. Open **Settings** by tapping your own avatar (your initial) at the top of your trips list or of a trip.
2. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account**.
3. The app asks “Delete your account?” and says:

   > Your name, photo and any trip only you were in are deleted. Messages and photos you sent stay with the people you sent them to, shown as from “Deleted account”. Expenses you paid for stay on shared trips — removing them would change what everyone else owes. This can’t be undone.

   Tap **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel**.

The account is deleted right away, and you’re signed out. This can’t be undone.

## Without the app

Email [privacy@traveltogether.now](mailto:privacy@traveltogether.now) from the email address you sign in with, and ask for your account to be deleted. It’s deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it’s done.

## What is deleted

Your email address and sign-in identities, your sessions, your push tokens and notification settings, your AI usage records, your feedback records in the app, and any trip that nobody else has been a member of. Your display name is replaced with “Deleted account” everywhere. One more case: if a trip has an open request to delete it and you’re the last member who hasn’t approved, deleting your account completes the request and the trip is deleted for everyone.

## What stays

What you shared stays with the people you shared it with, shown as from “Deleted account”: expenses, settlements, group chat messages, photos, notes, Bookings, and both sides of your direct messages. Removing them would change records other people rely on, such as what everyone owes. We keep them on the basis of legitimate interest. Direct message threads with a deleted account become read-only.

Feedback already copied to GitHub stays there. If you want specific content removed, such as a photo, a message or a GitHub feedback issue, email [privacy@traveltogether.now](mailto:privacy@traveltogether.now). Each request is handled by hand within 30 days.

Data sent to Anthropic for AI features is deleted by Anthropic within 30 days by default, as described in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for everything else.
