Plan, split, remember — together.

Travel Together is one app for a group trip. Split the bills, keep every booking and note in one plan, and talk it over in a chat that belongs to the trip.

Join the beta See how it works

For Android and iPhone, in closed beta.

Split the bill by what people actually had.

Scan a receipt and its lines become items. Tap who had what: the ramen goes to everyone, the beers to the three who drank them. Or split it evenly in one tap.

  • Each person’s share updates as you assign
  • Any currency, converted to the trip’s own
  • Fix the amount or the split later, from the expense

Settle up in as few payments as possible.

Travel Together keeps a running tally of who’s ahead and who’s behind. When the trip ends, it works out the shortest list of payments that squares everyone, so four people don’t have to send each other twelve transfers.

  • See how much each person put in
  • Mark a payment received when the money arrives
  • Everyone sees the same numbers, as they change

Bookings and notes, where everyone can find them.

The flight, the hotel and the dinner reservation sit in one plan with their confirmation numbers, so nobody has to dig through someone else’s inbox. Notes hold the rest: what to do on which day, and checklists anyone can tick off.

  • Today’s bookings show up on the trip’s home screen
  • Link a booking to a note, and its time stays current
  • Attach a screenshot or a PDF ticket

A chat that belongs to the trip.

Every trip has its own group chat, so the Osaka plans don’t get lost in the group you’ve had since school. Mention someone to get their attention, or message one person directly.

  • @mentions notify the person you named
  • Direct messages with anyone you travel with
  • New expenses and mentions collect in Activity

Claude helps, when you ask it to.

Three features use Claude, an AI made by Anthropic: reading a receipt photo, splitting a bill from a sentence (“Mei and I shared the gyoza”), and turning a few lines and some tickets into a plan. You see everything Claude produced before anyone else does.

  • Nothing is sent to Claude until you allow it
  • Anthropic doesn’t train on what you send
  • Turn it off at any time in Settings
